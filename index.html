📌 Part I
Varian's Advocacy

Varian, H. R. (1993). What use is economic theory? — Hal Varian presents the arguments in favor of using economic theory.

Discussion Question
According to you, what use is economic theory?
📊

1. Substituting for Data

Economic theory helps predict outcomes when direct data are lacking. When real-world data or empirical observations are unavailable or impossible to collect, theory provides a logical framework to infer and forecast what will happen.

🎯

2. Guiding the Identification of Key Parameters

Theory helps in identifying which parameters are important for understanding economic phenomena. Instead of examining every possible detail, economic models show researchers which specific variables and relationships are essential to measure.

🔗

3. Connecting Disparate Problems

Theory can reveal that seemingly different economic problems are related, providing a unifying framework. It demonstrates that different real-world situations often share the same underlying economic principles and mathematical structure.

💡

4. Generating Useful Insights

Even if a theory is not entirely accurate, it can still provide valuable insights into economic mechanisms that are useful in policy debates. Simplified models clarify key trade-offs and mechanisms even when they do not capture every real-world complexity.

🧮

5. Offering Quantification and Calculation

Theory provides a framework for quantifying relationships, which is essential for informed policy decision-making. It gives economists the tools needed to calculate costs, benefits, elasticities, and optimal outcomes.

🧠

6. Serving as an Antidote to Introspection

Theory helps overcome biases that arise from personal experience and introspection, leading to a more objective understanding of economic issues. It replaces personal feelings or personal anecdotes with strict logical reasoning.

🔬

7. Enabling Experimental Verification

Theory allows for the formulation of hypotheses that can be tested through experiments, refining theories and improving their real-world applicability. Clear theoretical predictions create testable claims that can be confirmed or disconfirmed by empirical data and experiments.

📌 Part II
Rubinstein's Dilemmas

Rubinstein, A. (2006). Dilemmas of an economic theorist. Econometrica, 865–883.

Discussion Question
Put yourself in the shoes of an economic theorist: what challenges do you face when presenting your work?
• Theory leads to absurd conclusions • Theory is rejected by the data • Empirics are enough to identify behavioral regularities • Theory is not relevant to the real-world issues
1
Rubinstein's Dilemma 1
The Dilemma of Absurd Conclusions
❓ Challenge

Should a model be discarded if it leads to absurd conclusions, or should it be seen as a limited tool that fails in some contexts?

✔ Resolution & Explanation

Economists should recognize that all models are simplifications of reality and will inevitably fail in some contexts. These absurdities highlight the limitations of the model's assumptions and serve as an opportunity to reinterpret them.

2
Rubinstein's Dilemma 2
The Dilemma of Responding to Evidence
❓ Challenge

Should economic models be adjusted or rejected if they are contradicted by experimental evidence?

✔ Resolution & Explanation

Economists should use empirical refutations as a basis for refining models or developing new ones. When experimental evidence contradicts a model, it shows us where the model needs improvement.

3
Rubinstein's Dilemma 3
The Dilemma of Modelless Regularities
❓ Challenge

Should economic theorists rely on models to discover regularities in human behavior, or can these regularities be identified directly through observation?

✔ Resolution & Explanation

Regularities can often be observed directly without the need for models. However, models can still play a useful role by providing a structured way to think about these regularities.

4
Rubinstein's Dilemma 4
The Dilemma of Relevance
❓ Challenge

Can economic theorists offer meaningful advice that impacts the real world, or are their models too detached from reality to be relevant?

✔ Resolution & Explanation

While economic theory may not always provide actionable advice, it is still relevant because it shapes the way people think about economic issues. Theorists should be responsible in how they present their work, recognizing the limitations of their models and avoiding oversimplification when addressing complex real-world problems.

Key Takeaway — 0 Introduction

Microeconomic theory provides useful tools to predict outcomes without data, guide parameter identification, connect disparate problems, and generate testable hypotheses (Varian). However, theorists face key dilemmas regarding absurd conclusions, empirical refutations, modelless regularities, and real-world relevance (Rubinstein). In this course, we will revise known concepts with proofs, introduce new concepts, and explore research combining theory and evidence.
